How It's Calculated
Every Game Counts, Forever
Every manager starts at 1500. After each regular-season or real-playoff game (consolation-bracket games don't count), both ratings shift based on the result — and on how surprising it was. Beating a team you were expected to beat barely moves the number; upsetting a stronger team moves it a lot. Blowouts move ratings more than nail-biters. Because it's calculated fresh from full match history each time, ratings carry across seasons automatically — there's no reset button, and no game ever stops mattering.
